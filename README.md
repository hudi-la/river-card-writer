# River

**A card-based writing workspace for collecting fragments, finding structure, and developing longer texts.**

### [Open River →](https://river.hudiworks.com/)

Created by [Hu Di](https://hudiworks.com/).

## Writing as something you can move around

River grew out of a practical need: ideas often arrive as scattered notes, quotations, and unfinished passages, while a finished piece needs an order that is still being discovered. River lets those fragments remain movable as the writing develops.

Keep material in card boxes, bring cards into a manuscript, and rearrange passages through an outline. Notes and longer-form writing stay alongside one another, so collecting, organizing, and composing can happen in the same workspace.

## In the workspace

- **Card boxes:** collect fragments and bring them into the main text when they become useful.
- **A movable outline:** navigate chapters and rearrange sections as the structure changes.
- **Writing and reading views:** edit the manuscript, preview its layout, and view Chinese and English side by side.
- **Version history:** revisit earlier versions as a piece develops.
- **Sharing and export:** share a text through a link or export the manuscript.
